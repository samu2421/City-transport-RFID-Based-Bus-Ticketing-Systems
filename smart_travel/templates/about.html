An RFID-based automated ticketing prototype for city buses.

Smart Travel replaces paper tickets and cash with a contactless card. Passengers tap an RFID card when they board and when they leave, and the fare is calculated from the distance between the two stops.

  • ESP32 microcontroller with MFRC522 RFID readers, one per bus stop, and green/red LEDs for feedback.
  • Card taps are sent over Wi-Fi to a Flask server that records the journey in a database.
  • Distance-based fare of Rs. 4 per kilometre, paid online through Razorpay.
  • Travel history for passengers and an admin panel to assign cards and manage users.

Built as a Project Based Learning-II project in the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, PES's Modern College of Engineering, Pune (2021–22).
