How it works

Tap in, tap out, pay online

1. Register

Create an account on this website. The administrator links an RFID card to your account.

2. Tap in when boarding

Scan your card at the reader of the stop where you board. A green LED confirms the entry.

3. Tap out when leaving

Scan the same card at the stop where you get off. The exit stop must be different from the entry stop.

4. Fare is calculated

The server works out the distance between the two stops and charges Rs. 4 per kilometre.

5. Pay online

Log in, open your profile and pay the outstanding fare through Razorpay. A receipt is shown once the payment is verified.

6. Travel again

The trip is added to your travel history and your card can be used for the next journey.
